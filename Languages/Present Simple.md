Formation:
- Affirmative: I/you/we/they/plural + base verb; he/she/it + base verb + s/es
- Negative: I/you/we/they + don't + base verb; he/she/it + doesn't + base verb
- Questions: Do + I/you/we/they + base verb; Does + he/she/it + base verb
Usage:
	Present simple describes habits, routines, general truths, and permanent states. Use it for actions that happen regularly or facts that are always true
Example:
- I work in Moscow (permanent state)
- She works every Sunday (routine)
- Do you like spicy food? (question)
- He doesn't do his homework (negative)
- The sun rises in the east (general truth)
