Formation:
- Affirmative: subject + am/is/are + verb + ing
- Negative: subject + am not/isn't/aren't + verb + ing
- Questions: Am/Is/Are + subject + verb + ing
Usage:
	Present Continuous describes actions happening right now, temporary situation, and future arrangements
Examples:
- She is studying at the moment (action now)
- They are living in London for a month (temporary)
- We are going to the cinema this weekend (future plan)
- I'm not sleeping now (negative)
- Is he eating now? (question)
