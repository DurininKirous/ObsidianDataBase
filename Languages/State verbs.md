List of words that typically use Present Simple instead of Continuous
### Mental states & Opinions
Thoughts: believe, know, understand, remember, forget, realize, recognize, suppose, mean, doubt, imagine, think, feel
Preferences & Emotions: like, love, hate, dislike, prefer, want, need, wish, hope, desire, adore, envy, fear, mind, care, value, appreciate

### Senses & Perception
appear, be, feel, hear, look, see, seem, smell, sound, taste, sense

### Possession & Relationship
belong, have (possession), own, possess, include, contain, consist, comprise, lack

### Measurement & Description
cost, measure, weigh, equal, resemble, fit, matter, depend

### Existence & Other States
be, exist, remain, involve, concern, deserve, owe, satisfy, promise, surprise, impress, please, astonish, agree, disagree, deny

### Important Note: Dual-Meaning Verbs
Some verbs can be BOTH stative and dynamic depending on meaning:​
- **have:** "I have a car" (possession - stative) vs "I'm having dinner" (activity - dynamic)
- **think:** "I think it's good" (opinion - stative) vs "I'm thinking about it" (mental process - dynamic)
- **see:** "I see the problem" (understand - stative) vs "I'm seeing her tomorrow" (meeting - dynamic)
- **taste:** "It tastes good" (perception - stative) vs "He's tasting the soup" (action - dynamic)
- **be:** "He is tall" (permanent - stative) vs "He's being rude" (temporary behavior - dynamic).
